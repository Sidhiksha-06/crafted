OUR STORY

We believe

WHY CRAFTED EXISTS

A product is more than
what it costs.

Behind every handmade object is time, skill, patience and a person who chose to make something with their hands.

Crafted was imagined as a digital space where that story doesn't disappear behind a product listing. We want buyers to discover the person, place and tradition behind what they choose to bring home.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Three ideas at
the heart of Crafted.

01

People first

We want the maker to be visible. Behind every product should be a person whose work deserves recognition.

02

Direct connection

We believe technology can make the relationship between independent makers and buyers more direct.

03

Craft has a future

Traditional craft doesn't have to stay in the past. It can evolve, reach new audiences and find new homes.
